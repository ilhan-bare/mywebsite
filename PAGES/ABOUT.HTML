Our Story

A family business built on trust, fragrance, and modest style.

History

Maniza Perfume is a family-run store trading near a busy taxi rank and mall precinct, where high daily foot traffic has built up a loyal walk-in customer base over the years. The shop specialises in oil-based Dudai perfumes — long-lasting, alcohol-free fragrances popular within the local Muslim community and beyond — alongside a curated range of Islamic clothing such as abayas, jilbabs, thobes, and prayer wear.

Word of mouth and repeat customers have kept the shop busy, but until now, all sales happened in person or through informal WhatsApp orders — with no formal online storefront to show the full range.
