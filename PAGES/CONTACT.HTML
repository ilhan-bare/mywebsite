Common Questions

FAQ

Are your perfumes really alcohol-free?

Yes — all our Dudai perfumes are oil-based and alcohol-free, making them halal-friendly and long-lasting on the skin.

How do I order?

Tap "Order Now" on any product to message us on WhatsApp with the item pre-filled, or use the enquiry form for a more detailed request.

Do you deliver, or is it collection only?

Both — we deliver locally around the taxi rank and mall area, and you're welcome to collect in person from either location.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Message us on WhatsApp within 3 days of collection or delivery and we'll help sort out a size exchange or return where possible.

Do you stock items not shown on the website?

Yes — our in-store range is larger than what's photographed so far. Ask us on WhatsApp and we'll send photos of anything you don't see online.
